# YouTube & video thumbnail prompts

Designed to read at phone-width. Big faces, high contrast, short on-image copy.  
**Recommended ratio:** 16:9 · **Export:** 2K minimum (thumbnails are heavily compressed)  
**Tool:** [Banana Pro AI](https://bananaproai.app/)

Always **quote exact title strings**. Prefer 3–5 words.

---

## 1. Shock / reaction face

```
Close-up of [PERSON DESCRIPTION: surprised creator, mid-30s, short dark hair]
facing camera, raised eyebrows, mouth open, [BG: bright yellow] solid background,
high contrast, YouTube thumbnail composition, 16:9, large empty space on the RIGHT
for title, sharp focus on eyes, dramatic rim light, no tiny details.
Text on image: "[TITLE: I WAS WRONG]" in huge bold white letters with thick black outline.
```

**Why it works:** Emotion + solid color + reserved text space + exact string.  
**Swap:** person, bg color, title; mirror space LEFT if you prefer.

---

## 2. Before / after split

```
Split-screen YouTube thumbnail 16:9: LEFT shows [BEFORE: dull messy desk, desaturated],
RIGHT shows [AFTER: bright organized desk, high saturation], bold vertical divider,
clear visual contrast, space at TOP for short title, no fine print.
Text: "[TITLE: FIX YOUR DESK]" bold sans-serif, top center, high contrast.
```

**Why it works:** Instant story without reading body copy.  
**Swap:** before/after scenes, title.

## 3. Tutorial object focus

```
Macro instructional shot of [OBJECT: hands holding a vintage film camera],
clean [BG COLOR: teal] backdrop, bright even light, 16:9, subject on LEFT third,
room for text on RIGHT, educational YouTube thumbnail, no busy patterns, no logos.
Text: "[TITLE: START HERE]" in bold cream letters with soft shadow.
```

**Why it works:** Object clarity + asymmetric layout for title.  
**Swap:** object, colors, title.

## 5. List / countdown

```
YouTube thumbnail 16:9: large numeral "[NUMBER: 7]" dominating LEFT side,
[SUBJECT: stack of books and laptop] on RIGHT, bold flat [BG: coral],
high contrast graphic design style, minimal photo noise, space for short subtitle.
Text: "[TITLE: HABITS]" bold white under the number.
```

**Why it works:** Numbers stop scrolling; flat bg keeps compression artifacts down.  
**Swap:** number, subject, colors.

## 8. Tech review “vs”

```
Split composition 16:9: LEFT [PRODUCT A: phone A], RIGHT [PRODUCT B: phone B],
VS badge in center circle, clean desk surface, bright even product lighting,
tech review thumbnail, both products same scale, high clarity.
Text: "[TITLE: A vs B]" bold white with dark outline, top center.
```

**Why it works:** Explicit “same scale” reduces unfair sizing hallucinations.  
**Swap:** products, title.

---

## Thumbnail checklist

- [ ] Face or hero object ≥ ~40% of frame  
- [ ] Title ≤ 5 words, quoted exactly  
- [ ] High contrast at 50% zoom  
- [ ] No more than two focal points  
- [ ] Safe margin from edges (platform UI crops)
