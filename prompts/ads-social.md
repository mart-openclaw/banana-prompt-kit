# Ads & social creative prompts

Feed, stories, and lightweight B2B ads. Message + product in one glance.  
**Ratios:** 1:1 feed · 9:16 stories/reels · 16:9 or 1.91:1-ish for LinkedIn/landscape  
**Tool:** [Banana Pro AI](https://bananaproai.app/)

Quote **exact claims**. Keep claims compliant for your market—this kit does not give legal advice.

---

## 1. Premium product feed ad (1:1)

```
Hero shot of [PRODUCT: reusable stainless water bottle in matte sage green],
soft gradient background sage-to-cream, centered, Instagram feed ad 1:1,
gentle contact shadow, premium eco brand feel, room ABOVE product for headline.
Text: "[CLAIM: Hydration, simplified.]" elegant sans-serif, dark gray.
```

**Why it works:** Gradient + margin for type + exact claim.  
**Swap:** product, gradient colors, claim.

## 2. Story / Reel vertical (9:16)

```

Gen-Z clean aesthetic, LARGE lower third empty for swipe CTA, no tiny text.
```

## Production notes

- Generate **without logo**, composite real marks in Figma/Canva.  
- Keep claims **provable**; avoid medical/financial guarantees.  
- For A/B tests, change **one** element (bg color *or* claim *or* crop).  
- Export 2K for most paid social; 4K if you crop heavily afterward.
